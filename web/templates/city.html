What separates a good provider from a cheap one

  • Licensing. Verify a current {{ STATE_NAMES.get(state, state) }} structural pest control license before signing anything.
  • Inspection before pricing. Any company quoting termite or bed bug work over the phone without inspecting is guessing. Reputable providers inspect first, and most inspect free.
  • Written scope and warranty. Termite treatments should come with a renewable damage or retreatment warranty. Get the term and the transfer terms in writing.
  • Recurring vs. one-time. Quarterly plans cost less per visit and prevent recurrence, but read the cancellation terms — annual auto-renew contracts are common.
  • Pets, kids, and sensitivities. Ask specifically about product classes and re-entry intervals rather than accepting a general "pet-friendly" claim.
